# ADR-002

## Identity, Authentication & Authorization

### Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Notify Engine V3 requires a consistent identity architecture supporting secure authentication and authorization across platform services, tenant services, administrators, and external integrations.

---

## Decision

Notify Engine V3 will adopt a **Federated Identity, Authentication & Authorization Architecture**.

Platform-wide identity principles shall govern authentication while authorization remains the responsibility of the owning service.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Consistent identity model.
* Strong access control.
* Independent authorization.
* Scalable governance.

### Trade-offs

* Requires identity lifecycle management.
* Requires governance discipline.
* Increased architectural coordination.
