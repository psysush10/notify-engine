# ADR-004

## Secrets, Key & Certificate Management

### Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Notify Engine V3 depends on sensitive security assets including secrets, cryptographic keys, certificates, and credentials that require consistent lifecycle management and governance.

---

## Decision

Notify Engine V3 will adopt a **Federated Secrets, Key & Certificate Management Architecture**.

Platform-wide governance shall establish lifecycle standards while Applications and Platform Modules remain responsible for managing their own security assets.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Secure lifecycle management.
* Clear ownership.
* Improved auditability.
* Technology independence.

### Trade-offs

* Requires lifecycle governance.
* Requires operational discipline.
* Increased architectural coordination.
